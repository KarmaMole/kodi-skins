0.9.7 (2026-10-21)
- Library panel: the library's own menu (View, Sort, Order, Watched, Search, Update library, Clean library,
  Library settings), in the System menu's text column. Reach it with Right on the last item of the index,
  Left / Right past the edges of the poster and cover walls, Left / Right past either end of the options row,
  or "More" at the end of that row. "↑ Options" in the library header says the row is there.
  Back closes it and leaves you on the item you came from.
- Clean library asks first ("No" is focused): Kodi's own CleanLibrary() would just run.
- View picker: "View" in the options row and in the panel opens a row of previews (Index, Wall, Covers; only
  the views that fit the folder), the current one marked; OK switches, Back keeps it. The previews are drawn
  from the views' geometry by _src/make_view_previews.py (no artwork).
- Home: the System entry (was Power) and its menu gain Update library and Clean library (Movies and TV or Music).
