  the season cards and the episode strip, or the new Options word in the header, unfolds a note with Update
  library (Stop the update while a scan runs), Clean library, View, Sort by, Order, Unwatched only, Search and

- The view self-correction timers no longer act while a dialog is open: with any note on top they read every
  view as hidden and could switch the library to List.
