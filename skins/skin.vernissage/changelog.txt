1.0.0 (2026-10-22) - first release
The whole skin: a private gallery that changes with the light (a Day room, four dusk steps, an Evening room), Home
with Tonight's exhibition, Still on the easel and New in the collection, the back office, the library in three views
(Salon, Gallery wall, Catalogue) with the curator's placard, the information pages, the listening room with
Visualiser mode and Presets, the video player, Search, every dialog and settings window. Grey prints and the easel
come from the optional KarmaMole Skin Helper (1.1.0); without it the walls fall back to Kodi's own lists.

Changes since the device audit:
- Album, artist and song placards in the library end on their last line; the rule with an empty line under it is
  gone (Kodi's music lists carry no track count).
- Times read without leading zeros: "1:01 of 48:12", "0:34", and h:mm:ss for an hour or more (the paused layer, the
  seek bar, the music transport and Visualiser band, the forked player strips).

Device audit fixes (M5, 2026-10-22, version stays 0.1.0)
- Dusk step 1: secondary text is darker (#46433F), so the Home hint and rail icons stay readable in the room's
  shaded corner.
- Series of works and series in the library: the placard no longer prints "2 of 7 seen" / "6 episodes to go" twice.
- Album information and the listening room: no stray " · " when an album has no record label (also artist years,
  styles and moods).
- Search: reopening it when the wall you left has no works for the kept query lands on the query line instead of
  leaving nothing in focus.
- Home without the KarmaMole Skin Helper: its lists no longer ask the missing add-on for items (no log errors); a
  series card on the easel shows its genres instead of repeating the year and network.

QA fixes (2026-10-21, version stays 0.1.0)
- "Acquired <month> <year>" reads the date in any region's format and hides when it can't (the premise takes the
  room).
- Listening room: Info shows only for songs in the library; Left / Right skip it otherwise.
- A premise cut at its last line ends in a fade and "…"; the same for the fullscreen info plot; the title's fade now
  covers the text.
