0.9.4 (2026-10-20)
- Install fix: the optional TMDb Helper dependency now accepts any version from 4.0.0, so the skin installs
  next to the 5.x TMDb Helper from Kodi's official repository ("unable to match dependency" on Kodi 21.3).

v0.9.3 (2026-10-19)
- Home card lift is smoother: both list layouts are the resting size (no row reflow); the lift is one zoom + tilt on one group, 180 ms sine out (was 280 ms back-overshoot).
- Lifted-card shadow is pre-sized and only fades (no scale); the old card's untilt is quicker (140 ms).
- The Home plot re-flows only once the row stops scrolling, then crossfades in.
- Row scroll 200 ms sine out; search card and cream cards lose their overshoot too.

v0.9.2 (2026-10-19)
- Clamped plots (spread, Home, episode list, concertina) end their last line with a fade and an ellipsis.
- Every clock follows the region time with AM/PM (System.Time, Player.FinishTime unformatted).
- Episode list crumb: the show once, then the season folder or the episode count.

v0.9.1 (2026-10-19)
- Spread: meta line with clean dots and minutes, one key-hint line, action words that always fit (Trailer case checked).
- OSD: audio / subtitle / video panel in a narrower font, shorter sliders and full tab names; no doubled dot when Previous/Next are
  hidden; "Bookmarks and chapters" instead of an empty chapters caption.
- Concertina panels carry their season (flattened series mix seasons). Card edges follow the section colour;
  header crumbs spaced; Kodi settings pages on a cream sheet; Back from Home row B goes straight to its tab.

v0.9.0 (2026-10-19)
- First build of Fold, forked from the Majan 1.0.4 engine.
- Home: die-cut section tabs, rows of closed cards that lift and tilt on focus, the focused card's info block,
  cream "continue" cards; sections stand up from the table when their tab is chosen.
- The card opens into a three-panel spread (two-phase hinge with perspective, fold shading in sync, from the slot
  it stood in); Back folds it closed.
- Libraries: the card shelf, season cards, the episode concertina, album sleeves, a cream list sheet.
- Albums open as a gatefold sleeve with the tracklist and the record half out; Now playing keeps it open.
- Player: a cream strip that folds up from the bottom edge with a perforated progress line and transport words.
- Dialogs are folded notes; keyboard and seek bar are strips; six skin settings including reduce motion.
