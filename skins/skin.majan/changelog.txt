1.0.5 (2026-10-20)
- Install fix: the optional TMDb Helper dependency now accepts any version from 4.0.0, so the skin installs
  next to the 5.x TMDb Helper from Kodi's official repository ("unable to match dependency" on Kodi 21.3).

v1.0.4 (2026-10-18)
- Video OSD: no rewind / fast-forward boxes (Left/Right seek without the OSD; Up on the row is the
  seek stepper). New order: Play/Pause, Stop, Previous, Next, Chapters | Subtitles, Audio, Video,
  Info, Player; the row wraps both ways.
- Music OSD: same treatment: Play/Pause, Stop, Previous, Next | Shuffle, Repeat, Queue; the row now
  wraps (Queue <-> Play), and the Play box gets the same 14 px gap as the other boxes.
- Counts read "1 track" / "1 item" instead of "1 tracks" / "1 items" (music OSD queue, track picker
  kicker, play queue page).

v1.0.3 (2026-10-18)
- Home spotlight: Left on Play shows the previous spotlight film, Right on the last button (Trailer, or
  Details when there's no trailer) shows the next one; wraps around the five.

v1.0.2 (2026-10-18)
- Titles: descenders (g, j, p, q, y) no longer clipped on the 2nd line of the Home spotlight title (display_100
  linespacing 0.74, unclipped visible copy), the info page, Showcase, music info and Now Playing.
- Play-start curtain: a black fade covers the page while the TV switches refresh rate at video start.
- Layout audit: focus frames no longer cut by containers (video/music OSD, player controls, music info
  buttons, add-on screenshots, Home hub rows, search artists, subtitle lists); text boxes sized to whole
  lines (no half-cut last line) across dialogs and settings; add-on info button row fits; settings and
  select dialog button rows fit three buttons; music artist stats row fits.
- Home spotlight column taller so the Play focus frame clears a 2-line title + 3-line plot.

v1.0.1 (2026-10-18)
- OSD auto-hide: new setting (Home & playback > OSD auto-hide: Off / 3 / 5 / 10 s, default 5 s);
  stays up while paused or while a menu is open.
- OSD Info button now opens the info screen (the OSD was swallowing the Info action).
- Info screen during playback: poster on the left; title, details, plot and media flags in a column of the same height (series poster for episodes).
- Artwork backgrounds: blurred fanart (poster fallback) at 480 px / radius 12 instead of a 240 px /
  radius 30 poster wash; levels raised to 30 / 45 / 65 %. Takes effect after a Kodi restart.
- Stronger, layered scrims behind the OSD and the info screen (taller bottom ramp, plateau, and a
  bottom-left corner scrim on the info screen) for legibility over bright frames.
- Context menu centred on screen.
- Home: the spotlight Play button focus frame is no longer clipped on the left.
- Skin settings: changing the spotlight interval keeps you on the Home & playback page.
- Spotlight rotation: actually rotates now; changing the interval applies immediately and keeps focus.
- Settings preview posters load reliably; seek bar condition fixed; empty-list focus fallbacks.

v1.0.0 (2026-10-18)
- Home: random spotlight of great unwatched films (rotating), newest row, menu-revealed hubs
  (Movies: recently added / newest / top rated unwatched; TV: next up + recently added episodes;
  Music: recently added / recently played / rediscover), search with auto-opening keyboard.
- Movies: Poster Showcase and Big Poster (5% focus zoom, dimmed neighbours); info page with cast,
  flags, optional trailer button (only when a trailer exists).
- TV: Wall with a Next up strip; episodes view.
- Music: Albums grid and Big Album (artist fanart, blurred-cover fallback); Now Playing and music OSD.
- Playback: OSD, seek bar with chapter ticks, track picker, subtitle download, tabbed audio/subtitle/
  video settings, minimal top offset strip.
- Settings: accent colours, rounded corners, focus outline, artwork background levels, watched markers,
  trailer button, spotlight rotation; system menu; minimal treatments for every other Kodi window.
- Plots auto-scroll when they don't fit; header tabs aligned with the date.
